# Forever merchant window
# Interface 16001.

GetMerchantItemInfo does not exist. Use C_MerchantFrame.GetItemInfo(index).
GetMerchantItemLink exists. GetMerchantNumItems is stock count, not how many
MerchantItemN frames are shown.

Slot widget: MerchantItemN is a Frame GetID=0.
Click target: MerchantItemNItemButton type=Button GetID=N.
Buyback: MerchantBuyBackItem + MerchantBuyBackItemItemButton. Do not scan as shop.
Tabs: Merchant id=1, Buyback id=2.

C_MerchantFrame.GetItemInfo fields (plain on this client):
  price            copper you PAY this merchant (listing)
  stackCount       pack size
  numAvailable     1 limited, -1 unlimited
  name, texture, isPurchasable, isUsable, hasExtendedCost, isQuestStartItem

Listing copper is also on MerchantItemNMoneyFrame gold/silver/copper buttons.
GetTitleText on MerchantFrame returns a FontString object, not a string.

Events: MERCHANT_SHOW, MERCHANT_UPDATE, MERCHANT_CLOSED, MERCHANT_FILTER_ITEM_UPDATE.
Hook MerchantFrame OnShow as well as MERCHANT_SHOW (the event may not run your
handler first).

EnumerateFrames can hit secret IsShown — skip secret booleans.

## Listing vs vendor sell

price on GetItemInfo (merchant) is what you pay the NPC.
C_Item.GetItemInfo sellPrice (return 11) is sell-to-vendor copper.

Token shop: listingPrice 0, hasExtendedCost true. Do not show Merchant: 0c;
show the token cost. BoP shop items (bindType 1) still show cost; do not AH-price them.

Forever can change shop prices, pack size, and limited stock. Cache last visit.

Live rows: Silver Contact 4404 listing 1s sell 25c limited.
Engineer's Ink 10647 listing 20s sell 500c unlimited.
Damaged Undermine Supply Crate 272151 token cost, bindType 1.
