# Forever Quest class vs quest-bound vs BoP
# Interface 16001. Live C_Item.GetItemInfo.

Do not treat classID 12 / itemType "Quest" as "cannot vendor / cannot AH".
Forever files many gather and quest-turn-in mats as Quest/Quest even when they
are unbound, have a vendor price, and post on the auction house.

## Key off bind, not class

bindType 0 none     can vendor / AH if it has a price
bindType 1 pickup   BoP. Do not post. Vendor copper may still be > 0.
bindType 2 equip / 3 use  not the quest-item problem
bindType 4 quest    real quest-bound. Do not post.

Tooltip when present:
  "Quest Item" (ITEM_BIND_QUEST) — real quest item
  Soulbound / ITEM_BIND_ON_PICKUP — do not AH-price it

Do not skip just because classID 12, itemType "Quest", or "used by a quest".

C_Item.GetItemInfo on this client returns 18 values (wiki lists 17).
Index 11 is sellPrice (sell-to-vendor copper, per item).
Index 14 is bindType.
Index 18 can be flavor text (not always empty).

Blizzard Sell Price / tooltip GetMoney is the hovered STACK. Per-item vendor
is GetItemInfo sellPrice. Compare stack math to the money line.
