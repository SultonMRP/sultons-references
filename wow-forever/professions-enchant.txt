# Forever professions / replace-enchant popup
# Interface 16001.

Enchant button: ProfessionsFrame.CraftingPage.CreateButton (often no GetName).
Enchanting professionID 333, Enum.Profession 9.

REPLACE_TRADESKILL_ENCHANT is the "replace Intellect +2 with Stamina +3?" Yes.
EQUIP_BIND_TRADEABLE is a different loot dialog. Do not auto-yes that one.

StaticPopup_OnClick(dialog, 1) -> ReplaceTradeskillEnchant() is allowed only
during the player's Enchant click (hardware stack).

CreateButton:Click() after C_Timer / UNIT_AURA can still CraftEnchant, then
Yes is ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED. Same as chat: two protected calls in one click
work; a timer between them does not.

CancelUnitBuff does not drop Cat Form (768) / Bear Form (5487) / Dire Bear (9634).
Secure /cancelaura does. Do not unshift from Lua then click Enchant.
Player unshifts, then clicks Enchant, then Yes on the same click is fine.
