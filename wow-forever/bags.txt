# Forever bag slots
# Interface 16001. Combined bags.

Default bag item buttons are CheckButtons. Do not skip every CheckButton
(bag *toggles* in some bag addons are also CheckButtons — skip those only when
there is no GetSlotAndBagID / item link).

itemButton:GetSlotAndBagID() returns slot, bag (same order CanIMogIt uses).
GetID / GetBagID alone is wrong on combined bags.

Walk:
  ContainerFrameCombinedBags.Items
  ContainerFrameContainer.ContainerFrames[].Items
  ContainerFrameN.Items and ContainerFrameNItemM
  BankFrame.BankPanel:FindItemButtonByBankTabAndContainerSlotID

Overlay frames that SetAllPoints the slot (CanIMogIt CIMI_AddToFrame and similar)
sit on top of a FontString parented to the item button. Letters / tags vanish
even when you painted. Raise your holder: child Frame on the button,
SetFrameLevel(button+30), FontString OVERLAY.

Bagnon / BagBrother: Item:Update. Do not use Bag:GetBagID as a slot.
Baganator: RegisterCornerWidget.
MyBags: reparents combined bag slots; hook ContainerFrameCombinedBags.UpdateItemLayout.
