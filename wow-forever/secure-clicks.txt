# Forever secure clicks / modifiers
# Interface 16001. Pair with client.txt secrets and combat.

A parent that owns SecureActionButtons is protected.
Lua Show / Hide / SetAttribute during InCombatLockdown taints.
Use SecureHandlerStateTemplate + RegisterStateDriver, then Show/Hide again
on PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED.

Unattended spell or item use is still automation. Prefer click or bound key.
Unprotected BackdropTemplate panels, custom buttons, sliders may Show/Hide/SetPoint
in combat. Do not wrap those in InCombatLockdown.

## Modifier + bag click (DE / mill / salvage overlay)

Overlay a SecureActionButton on the hovered bag item while a modifier is held.
Do not UseContainerItem from the tooltip hook.

RegisterAttributeDriver(btn, "visibility", "[mod:alt] show; hide")
Defer that call if InCombatLockdown (queue PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED).

IsAltKeyDown / IsShiftKeyDown can be secret. The [mod:alt] driver is the
hardware-state path. Lua IsAltKeyDown in a tooltip hook may fail closed.

SetAttribute target-bag / target-slot from ItemLocation when not in combat.
The tooltip hook only positions the overlay; the click is the secure button.

Never WorldFrame / UIParent OnMouseDown or GLOBAL_MOUSE_DOWN (ore click + camera).

Dead / ghost: pcall UnitIsDeadOrGhost / UnitIsGhost; issecretvalue first.
UnitIsFeignDeath plain true means they are not dead (Hunter FD).
A ghost is often not in InCombatLockdown. Do not use UnitHealth == 0.
