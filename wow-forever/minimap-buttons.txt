# Forever minimap buttons
# Interface 16001.

SexyMap and MinimapButtonButton collect buttons named LibDBIcon10_* parented
to Minimap. A button parented to UIParent is invisible to those collectors
even if it looks glued to the minimap.

Use LibDBIcon-1.0 + LibDataBroker-1.1 (LibStub + CallbackHandler).
Do not vendor LibDBIcon alone.

Addon compartment / minimap: same LibDBIcon register path.
